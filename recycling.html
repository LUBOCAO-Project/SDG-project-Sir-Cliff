RECYCLING PROGRAM
Manolo Fortich came together with one mission to protect and preserve our environment through a united clean-up drive. With volunteers of all ages working hand in hand, this simple act of service became a powerful reminder that real change begins with us. By cleaning our surroundings and caring for our shared spaces, we continue to build a cleaner, greener, and more responsible Manolo Fortich for future generations.
The Immaculate Church community in Manolo Fortich came together in a meaningful clean-up drive, showing that faith shines brightest through action. With united hands and committed hearts, volunteers worked to keep the surroundings clean and cared for, reminding everyone that protecting our environment is also an act of service to God and to one another.
United by purpose and care for the environment, Manolo Fortich gather along the riverbanks to remove waste and restore the natural flow of the water, showing that a cleaner, healthier river is possible when the community works hand in hand.
